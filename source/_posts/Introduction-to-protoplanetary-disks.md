## Structure

![structure of protoplanetary disk (Miotello et al., 2022)](fig1.png)

- Mass:
  dust: $1\sim100M_\oplus$
  gas: $10^{-5}\sim10^{-1}M_\odot$
  $m_\text{gas}/m_\text{dust}\sim 10^{2}$

- Size:
  $\sim 100\text{AU}$
  $r_\text{gas}/r_\text{dust}\sim 2$

Channel map: images with varing $\lambda$
