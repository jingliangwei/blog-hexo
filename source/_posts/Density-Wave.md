Lin, C. C., & Shu, F. H. (1964). On the spiral structure of disk galaxies. *The Astrophysical Journal*, *140*, 646. https://doi.org/10.1086/147955

(林家翘, 徐遐生)

<!-- more -->

The density wave has the form
$$
\mu'(r,\theta,t)=S(r)e^{-\omega_i t}\cos[\omega_r t-n\theta+\Phi(r)]\tag{6}
$$

If $S(r)$ varies slowly while $\Phi(r)$ varies quickly, then Eq.(6) gives a spiral structure (phase contour):
$$
\omega_r t-n\theta+\Phi(r)=\text{const.}
$$
at a fixed time $t$, these $n$ arms in the spiral satisfy
$$
\theta=\frac{1}{n}[\Phi(r)+\text{const.}]\tag{7}
$$
